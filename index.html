Geological Profile & Eruption History

Mayon Volcano is famous for its nearly perfect conical shape. It is the most active volcano in the Philippines, having erupted over 50 times in the past 400 years. Its explosive eruptions often generate deadly pyroclastic flows, mudflows (lahars), and ash plumes.
